**The upgrade pattern is M002's own, and the case asserts both halves of it.**

link a bibliographic listing, so moving a claim onto a Tier-A Further Reading

scope rather than source it — an evasion that looks exactly like a fix. That is
why `S02-F18` in `citation-tier.test.mjs` asserts the old superscript is gone
and the new anchor present, and why this slice follows suit.
