F43's item overstates what was missing. Rule 6 already stated the formula as
`3 × phrase + 2 × gap ≡ remaining matras (mod cycle)` and already cited
[Nelson 2008](/appendix-references/#fr-nelson-2008). What was missing was that
nobody worked it.

The numbers come from the page's own patch rather than being invented: the
`Tihai phrase` lane carries `Phrase Len` 5 and `Gap` 0.5 against a 16-matra
cycle, and `3 × 5 + 2 × 0.5 = 16` closes it exactly, so the third statement's
final stroke lands on sam. The prose also shows the failure case — a gap of 1
needs 17 matras and overshoots — because Rule 6's own point is that a tihai
missing sam is failed rather than loose.

The case derives all three numbers from the lane and never carries its own copy
of 5, 0.5 or 16. It reads only the prose *after* the last table row, so the
table the numbers came from cannot satisfy the assertion by itself.

Watched to fail three times:

- before the prose existed → `the prose beside the patch does not print 5, 0.5,
  16 (3 × 5 + 2 × 0.5 = 16)`
- with the printed product changed 16 → 15 → `does not print 16`
- **with the lane's `Gap` changed 0.5 → 1** → `which does not close the lane's
  16-matra cycle`

The third is the one that matters. It is what proves the prose is coupled to the
table rather than to a constant, which is the difference between an arithmetic
example that is checked and one that is merely printed — the row's second
definition-of-done item.
