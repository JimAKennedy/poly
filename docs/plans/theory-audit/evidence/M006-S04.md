**No prose changed.** The row asked whether *kotekan polos* has an attestation,
and the answer is that the term does not denote what the audit says it denotes,
so there is nothing to attest.

The audit (§125) calls it "a third player playing only the structural pokok
tones". Sources agree that *polos* is one of the two interlocking kotekan parts
— the on-beat one, paired with *sangsih* — and that the *pokok* is the main
melody, played on calung and ugal, which kotekan embellishes. Polos does not
play the pokok; it is half of what ornaments it.

M003/S05 refused the label: adopting it would have collided with the page's
established usage. That refusal was correct, on evidence that did not exist when
it was made.
