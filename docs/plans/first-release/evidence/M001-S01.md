with a fresh page load and no mode chip clicked.

The case bites — the gating was restored in `ui.css` (`display: none`), the spec
went red, and the file was confirmed byte-identical after reverting.

left `ui.js`, and `#capCtl` became `display: flex` unconditionally in `ui.css`
with the `.show` rule deleted. The `role="group"` and `aria-label` were already
correct and were not touched.
