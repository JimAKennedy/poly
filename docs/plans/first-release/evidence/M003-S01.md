same journal, and the claim helper searches the whole file rather than the

That is the identical shape to `REF6-JONES`, where `presentRegex` had to pin the

| mutation | result |
|---|---|

| the JSTOR link is removed | **red** |

The lesson is worth carrying into M003/S03: a guard over a bibliography has to
say *which entry*, because a bibliography is full of entries that look alike.
