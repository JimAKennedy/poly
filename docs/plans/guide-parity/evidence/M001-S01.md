**The coefficients are stated as ours.** The comment says the curve reproduces
the *shape* Friberg & Sundström (2002) report and that the values are Poly's,
so refining them against a source in hand is a data change needing no code.
That is the framing M003 used for the jembe profile, and claiming these were
measured values would be the citation failure the theory-audit programme
existed to remove.

`swingOffsetFraction` is separate from the ratio deliberately: the ratio is the
musical claim and the displacement is its consequence, and at a 2:1 ratio the
fraction agrees with what the fixed mapping already produces — the two paths
meet where they should.
