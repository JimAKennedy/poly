**Reich Phasing, and the reason it is the right preset.** Its two lanes are
identical at pitch 76 with one drifting — Reich's phasing *rhythm* without its
substance, because the technique works on a **pitched** figure. Piano Phase and
Violin Phase are two players on the same short melodic cell, one drifting out of
phase. Both lanes now carry the same five-note cell and lane 1's `driftRate`
does what the second performer does. A sequence on a kick lane would have
demonstrated the field while misrepresenting the preset.

The cell is ours, in the spirit of the piece rather than a transcription of it —
the framing the samba and jembe profiles already carry.

**The byte-identity golden fired, and was reconsidered rather than weakened.**
M003/S01 wrote it as "no preset ships a sequence", true then and deliberately
designed to fail the moment one did. It did. The claim worth protecting is that
a lane *without* a sequence is unmoved — a lane with one is supposed to sound
different — so the sweep now renders every preset twice, as shipped and with
sequences cleared, and asserts onsets never move and unsequenced lanes keep
their pitch. It also asserts at least one preset *does* carry a sequence, so the
coexistence claim is not made against a tree where none exist.

**Two mistakes engine-capability M003/S01 made, avoided here.** The
`schemaVersion` was raised in the emitter's **doc comment and the string it
writes** together — that slice raised only the comment and the generator's gate
caught it. And `presets-json-schema.test.mjs` was updated in the same commit —
that slice missed it entirely, because it runs under `site-unit` and that slice
owed only engine tokens.

`presets.json` reads `schemaVersion: 7`, with Reich Phasing lanes 0 and 1 both
carrying `[76, 78, 81, 78, 76]`.
