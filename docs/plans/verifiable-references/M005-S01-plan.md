## Why it is accepted

- **The archive prefix exists.** M003 named every Further Reading file
  `FR - Surname Year.pdf`, derived from its anchor, so the convention has a
  prefix for every entry without a number.
- **A reader can already cite one.** Further Reading entries are cited by
  author and year, linked to their anchor, which the shipping chapters do 33
  times.
- **The merge does not need it.** M005/S02 makes each bibliography one list
  with numbered entries keeping `[N]` and Further Reading staying
  author–year; nothing is renumbered, so the third DoD item holds trivially
  and the second is S02's to prove.
