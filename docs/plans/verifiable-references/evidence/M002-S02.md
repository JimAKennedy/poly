Musical Rhythms", *Proceedings of BRIDGES*, and links `arxiv.org/pdf/0705.4085.pdf`.

Distance Geometry of Music" by Demaine, Gomez-Martin, Meijer, Rappaport,

`arXiv:0705.4085v1 [cs.CG] 28 May 2007`. Toussaint is one of eight authors, not
the sole author, and the year and title are both different.

Both works are real and both discuss Euclidean rhythms, so the link is not
useless — but the citation describes a different work from the one it points at.
The archived copy is byte-identical to what the URL serves, so this is a
citation defect and not a mis-download.
