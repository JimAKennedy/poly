Written test-first: the new `M003-S03-jembe-profile` claim failed against the
unedited page, naming the forbidden phrase. Proved again after the rewrite by
restoring the disclaimer and watching it fail — **restored by an explicit edit
rather than `git checkout`**, which in M007/S03 discarded uncommitted work in the
same breath.

Construction step 5 said "Poly's per-step micro-timing offsets are where such a
profile would be entered... what Poly does not ship is a measured jembe profile
to put in them." Both halves were wrong by the end of this slice: Poly ships the
profile, and micro-timing was never the right place for it. The paragraph itself
calls the profile "particular ratios, stable across tempi", and micro-timing is
absolute milliseconds clamped to ±20 — at 90 BPM a sixteenth is about 167 ms, so
a ratio encoded there survives exactly one tempo.

The rewrite keeps the page's existing and correct three-way distinction and
sharpens it: **profile** is the meter's shape, **micro-timing** a player's lean,
**humanize** random jitter. Two older locks constrain that paragraph —
`S04-F31` requires "random jitter" and "systematic profile", `S04-B10` requires
"micro-timing" — and all three phrases survive, so neither lock had to move.
That is the difference from S02, where a lock did have to move and the move was
recorded.
