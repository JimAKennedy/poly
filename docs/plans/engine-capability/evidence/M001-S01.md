Hits-per-step is not a comparable rate on this page: lanes 6 and 7 run at 1/16
while the rest run at 1/4, so the same step count covers a quarter of the time.
The predicate computes hits per whole note instead, from `Steps` and
`Subdivision` together.

same-register instruments, and Rule 9 places the kotekan at the top of the
pyramid as one stratum rather than two. The predicate treats equal registers as
passing, so this is the tie the rule permits rather than an exemption.

voice at the top and the fastest at the bottom — failed with `Kenong (note 43)
sits above Polos (note 36) but plays sparser`. Reverted.
