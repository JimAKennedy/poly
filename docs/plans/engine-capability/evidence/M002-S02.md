like a weak predicate. It was neither: `gam-structural-overlap` is registered on
`05-gamelan.mdx`, and `theory-gamelan`'s Rule 4 triage *points* at it — the
cross-page arrangement where the chapter's patch is the worked example. Mutating
the theory page could not move a check that reads the chapter page. Proved
against the right table instead: reverting the chapter patch to `nyogcag` with
overlap 0 fails with the empty-intersection message.

**That left the theory page's new columns checked by nothing**, which is how a
table drifts from the rule it illustrates. The derivation was factored into a
shared `kotekanPairOverlap` helper and registered for *both* patches, with the
theory page's case named in its triage entry. Both were mutation-proved
separately.

`empat` with overlap 0 was also tried and **passes** — the style shares step 5
with the polos on its own, so the intersection does not depend on the overlap
count. That is worth knowing: it is why the preset uses `empat` rather than
`telu`, which at E(5,8) is the strict complement.

Both tables now carry `Interlock` and `Overlap` columns, and
`05-gamelan.mdx`'s patch declares `preset="Balinese Kotekan"`, so its values
match what the preset ships.

Rule 4's parenthetical — "add deliberate doublings via a third lane or accent
masks until kotekan modes ship" — is gone, replaced by a sentence saying the
