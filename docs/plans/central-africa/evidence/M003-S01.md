**Claims about Poly checked against the tree.** The rules name only controls
the parameter appendix lists: Emphasis, Swing, Humanize, Drift Rate, Mutation,
Probability, phrase gating, Tempo Mult, Cell Sizes, Scene A and B, Chain.
Poly has no per-step accent mask, so R8 says to give accented strokes their own
lane, not to use one. R10's "the global macros leave a timeline lane alone"
is `resolveMacros` skipping any lane with `timeline` set; a lane's own Mutation
and Drift are not macros and still apply.

**Counts on the page.** N5's "21 or 22 of their 24 positions" and "0 to 2
shared by every part" are the four-part Aka formulae's count rows (mò.kóndí
21 and 1, yómbè 22 and 0, mò.nzòlì 22 and 0, mò.mbénzélé 21 and 2). N6's
dotted quarter of 138 to 160 is exx. 102, 104, 106, 108, 111, 112 and 115; a
24-value cycle at those tempi lasts 3.0 to 3.5 seconds.
