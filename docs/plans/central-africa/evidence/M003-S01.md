**Claims about Poly checked against the tree.** The rules name only controls
the parameter appendix lists: Emphasis, Swing, Humanize, Drift Rate, Mutation,
Probability, phrase gating, Tempo Mult, Cell Sizes, Scene A and B, Chain.
Poly has no per-step accent mask, so R8 says to give accented strokes their own
lane, not to use one. R10's "the global macros leave a timeline lane alone"
is `resolveMacros` skipping any lane with `timeline` set; a lane's own Mutation
and Drift are not macros and still apply.

**Counts on the page.** N5's "21 or 22 of their 24 positions" and "0 to 2
shared by every part" are the four-part Aka formulae's count rows (mò.kóndí
21 and 1, yómbè 22 and 0, mò.nzòlì 22 and 0, mò.mbénzélé 21 and 2). N6's
dotted quarter of 138 to 160 is exx. 102, 104, 106, 108, 111, 112 and 115; a
24-value cycle at those tempi lasts 3.0 to 3.5 seconds.

**Audit targets against N5.** Step 5 asks for 20 or more of 24 positions
marked, no more than two shared by every part, and a solo position for each
part. N5 on the page gives 21 or 22 marked and 0 to 2 shared by every part
for the four-part Aka formulae, so each meets the first two targets; the
three-part ngbɔ̀lù marks 20 and shares 2.

**What breaks the idiom, against the vision's CA3 list.** The vision asks for
"what breaks the idiom, including the global macros". The page lists co-prime
lengths (R7), step 1 and Emphasis (R6), Mutation and Drift on marked lanes
(R10), the global macros on Euclidean lanes (R10), Swing and Humanize (R1),
vertical alignment and unmarked streams (R8, R9), and spread variation (R12).
The macro item was checked against `resolveMacros`: it skips timeline lanes;
Complexity and Density recompute Hits; Complexity and Syncopation change
Rotation; Swing and Humanize add to each lane's swing and humanize. At the
presets' macro settings (complexity and density 0.5, the rest 0) every
Euclidean lane keeps its recipe, which M002's engine grid test proves.
