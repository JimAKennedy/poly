## Locke 1982, read before planning

`FR - Locke 1982.pdf` from the owner's archive, read as page images on
2026-10-18 (`pdftotext` clips line ends on this scan). What it supports about
Ewe cycle lengths, quoted in the plan with pages: the bell establishes the
basic musical period (p. 217); in Atsiagbeko the gankogui, axatse, totodzi and
kloboto "share the same phrase length and begin together", the kagan "must
cycle 4 times per bell pattern" and the kidi "twice" (p. 219); a cross-rhythm's
period "may be shorter than the time span of the bell pattern" (p. 233); the
common cross-rhythms are 3:2 and 3:4, with 3:8 sometimes (p. 244). Nothing in
it supports parts of 7 or 5 pulses against a 12-pulse bell, so CA30 presents
12/7/5 as Poly's extension.
