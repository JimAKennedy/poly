- hit counts within their cycles
- output in the first bar

**The bòndò count: a correction to the vision's reasoning.** The vision kept
bòndò's three drums as three lanes because "Arom's count of 3 at those
positions is three tone colours sounding together". That is wrong. Arom's
`(count)` row counts *parts*, and the three drums are one part on one model
rhythm (exx. 101–103); the 3 at position 1 is the drums, the dì.kétɔ̀ and the
rattle. Summed by lane, three drum lanes give 5 there. The harness now lets
one part span several lanes: every lane must sound the part's row exactly, and
a position counts once per part. The three lanes and their three tone colours
stay, as the owner chose, and Arom's count row is matched exactly.

**Presets 46–55:** Aka: Mokondi, Yombe, Monzoli, Mombenzele, Zoboko, Bondo,
Ngbolu Drums; Banda-Dakpa: Gaza; Zande: Kponingbo; Banda-Linda: Aga-torumo.

- Kponingbo and Bondo have no drawn lane. The factory referent lock fixes their
